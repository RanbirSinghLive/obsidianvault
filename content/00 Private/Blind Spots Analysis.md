## Blind Spot #3: The Hidden Perfectionism Rule—"Ship When Perfect or Don't Ship At All"

**The contradiction:** Professionally, you thrive on iteration—PAL expansion in 30 days, pilots launched ahead of schedule. You move **fast** under pressure.

## The Synthesis

All three stem from the same root: **You've applied the frameworks that made you successful in optimization-driven work to creative writing, where that framework is fundamentally mismatched.**

- Professional strength: *Constraints + systems = leverage*
- Creative writing requires: *No constraints + permission to fail = discovery*

You measure creative work like business deals: completeness, efficiency, risk mitigation. But a novel isn't complete when the outline is perfect—**it's complete when it's published and in readers' hands**.

At 35, with 15 years invested in *The Flamingos Fly*, you're running out of time to discover that the only way out is to **write badly, ship imperfectly, and iterate from reader feedback**—not endless private refinement.
