## Filing Deadlines

**Individual Tax Return:**
- Federal: April 30, 2026
- Quebec: April 30, 2026

**Self-Employed:**
- Filing deadline: June 15, 2026
- Payment deadline: April 30, 2026 (interest applies after this date)

## Preparation Tips

1. **Organize by category** - Keep documents separated by income, deductions, credits
2. **Digital copies** - Scan or photograph all receipts and documents
3. **CRA My Account** - Check for missing slips at canada.ca/my-cra-account
4. **Revenu Québec** - Access "My Account for citizens" at revenuquebec.ca
5. **Keep records** - Retain all documents for 6 years after filing
6. **Auto-fill** - Use CRA and Revenu Québec auto-fill services when filing

## Tax Software Recommendations

- **TurboTax** - User-friendly, handles Quebec returns

- **Studio Tax** - Free option
- **GenuTax** - Free, supports Quebec

**Note:** All major Canadian tax software handles both federal and Quebec returns simultaneously.

---

## Important Quebec vs Federal Differences

- Quebec requires separate provincial return (not integrated like other provinces)
- Different credit amounts and rules (childcare, medical, donations)
- Quebec has unique credits: solidarity tax credit, work premium
- QPIP vs EI parental benefits have different tax treatment
- Quebec tuition credits calculated differently
